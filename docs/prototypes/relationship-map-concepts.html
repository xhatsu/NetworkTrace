Relationship map concepts

Four ways to show who reaches what across 200+ services without drawing everything at once. Every prototype below runs on the same mocked estate, so you can compare them on equal terms. Click around: they are working views, not pictures.

Rule for every concept≤ 30 nodes on screenComplexity is bounded by choosing a level or an anchor, never by cutting to the top N of everything.
Idea

One entity in the centre with one hop on each side. Clicking a neighbour moves the centre there. You walk the graph instead of looking at all of it.

Answers

"What does this user reach?" "Who calls this API, and through which service?"

Why it scales

The screen shows at most 10 neighbours per side. The rest sit in a searchable list, so nothing folds into "Other".

Weak spot

No estate-wide picture. You need a starting point: search, a feed item or a heatmap cell.

Idea

A heatmap that follows the service catalog: group to group, then service to service inside one cell, then users × APIs for one service pair.

Answers

"Which parts of the estate talk to each other?" "Where is the unknown traffic?" "What changed this week?"

Why it scales

A matrix has no crossing lines. Each level shows at most about 20 × 20 cells, whatever the estate size.

Weak spot

Needs real catalog groups. With one group (today's live data) the top level is a single cell.

Idea

No graph on the landing page. A ranked list of relationship changes, each with its path written out, and an estate table by group beside it.

Answers

"What needs my attention?" New reach, silent paths, surges, APIs dominated by unknown users.

Why it scales

The number of changes grows far slower than the number of services. 200 or 2,000 services is still one list.

Weak spot

Shows only what changed. A quiet but odd relationship stays invisible until you look for it.

Idea

The whole estate as one icicle: Group → Service → API, sized by traffic. Pick a user and their reach lights up. Click a block to zoom in.

Answers

"How much of the estate does this user touch?" "How is traffic spread across groups and services?"

Why it scales

A partition has no edges at all. 240 services is a column of slices, readable after one zoom.

Weak spot

Shows reach but not the path. Caller services are not drawn.

Recommendation

Build one page from three of them. C opens the page, with B's group heatmap at the top as the overview. Every item in the feed and every heatmap cell opens A, the anchor explorer, for the details.
